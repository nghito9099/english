01 · Warm-up

Why do we love cartoons?

🎨 A world of creativity

Cartoons build imaginative worlds with unique characters, colors, music, and stories.

💛 Feelings we understand

They help us explore friendship, courage, family, kindness, and growing up.

🌍 Stories for everyone

Animation crosses language and cultural barriers. A great story can connect people everywhere.

🧠 More than entertainment

Many cartoons encourage creativity, problem-solving, empathy, and critical thinking.

“A cartoon can make an imaginary world feel surprisingly real.”

03 · Story spotlight

Steven Universe

2013–2019FantasyComing-of-age

Created by Rebecca Sugar, this series follows Steven, a kind-hearted boy who lives with the Crystal Gems: Garnet, Amethyst, and Pearl.

As Steven learns about his powers and his family, he also discovers that understanding others can be more powerful than fighting them.

What makes it special?

  • Complex characters who learn from mistakes
  • Music and emotional storytelling
  • Messages about empathy and accepting differences
03 · Steven Universe

Meet the Crystal Gems

Steven

Optimistic and caring. He tries to solve problems by listening and understanding.

Garnet

Calm and confident. She represents trust, strength, and commitment.

Amethyst

Funny and spontaneous. She learns to value herself and her own identity.

Pearl

Organized and thoughtful. Her journey explores grief, loyalty, and independence.

Key idea: Being different does not make someone less valuable.

04 · Story spotlight

Adventure Time

2010–2018FantasyAdventure

Set in the colorful Land of Ooo, the show follows Finn, a brave human boy, and Jake, his magical dog and best friend.

Together, they meet unusual creatures, explore strange places, and face challenges that are often funny but sometimes surprisingly thoughtful.

What can we learn?

  • Friendship means supporting each other
  • Courage is acting even when things are difficult
  • Growing up means accepting change
05 · Story spotlight

The Powerpuff Girls

1998–2005SuperheroComedy

Blossom, Bubbles, and Buttercup are three super-powered sisters created by Professor Utonium. They protect Townsville from villains while handling ordinary childhood problems.

Three different strengths

  • Blossom: leadership and strategy
  • Bubbles: kindness and optimism
  • Buttercup: bravery and determination

Their differences become their greatest strength when they work together.

06 · Story spotlight

Masha and the Bear

2009–presentComedyFamily

This cheerful animated series follows Masha, an energetic little girl, and Bear, a patient former circus performer who enjoys a quiet life in the forest.

Masha's curiosity often turns an ordinary day into a funny adventure. Bear usually helps her learn from the consequences of her actions.

Its special charm

  • Expressive physical comedy
  • Simple stories that are easy to follow
  • Lessons about patience, friendship, and responsibility

A little chaos, a lot of heart

Even when Masha makes a mess, the friendship between the two characters remains at the center of the story.

Illustrative character motif
