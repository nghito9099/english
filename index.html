01 · Warm-up

Why do we love cartoons?

🎨 A world of creativity

Cartoons build imaginative worlds with unique characters, colors, music, and stories.

💛 Feelings we understand

They help us explore friendship, courage, family, kindness, and growing up.

🌍 Stories for everyone

Animation crosses language and cultural barriers. A great story can connect people everywhere.

🧠 More than entertainment

Many cartoons encourage creativity, problem-solving, empathy, and critical thinking.

“A cartoon can make an imaginary world feel surprisingly real.”

03 · Steven Universe · 01

Steven Universe

💎 CARTOON NETWORK

A beloved American animated television series created by Rebecca Sugar.

2013–20195 seasons160 episodesScience fantasy

The story follows Steven Universe, a half-human, half-Gem boy living in Beach City with the Crystal Gems: Garnet, Amethyst, and Pearl.

A story about growing up, discovering who you are, and learning to understand other people.

03 · Steven Universe · 02

Steven's world & the Crystal Gems

Steven

Kind-hearted and curious. He learns to control the powers inherited from his mother, Rose Quartz.

Garnet

Calm and powerful, representing trust, confidence, and emotional strength.

Amethyst

Funny and spontaneous. Her story explores self-worth and accepting your own identity.

Pearl

Thoughtful and disciplined. Her journey explores love, grief, loyalty, and independence.

03 · Steven Universe · 03

Why it matters

Steven Universe is praised for combining fantasy with meaningful stories about emotional intelligence, self-acceptance, empathy, healthy relationships, and LGBTQ+ representation.

🧠 Emotional intelligence

Characters learn to recognize feelings and communicate instead of simply fighting.

💛 Self-acceptance

The story encourages people to understand their own identity and value.

Key takeaway: empathy can be a superpower.

The Powerpuff Girls

💥 SUPERHERO

A classic American animated series created by Craig McCracken.

Premiered: 1998Cartoon NetworkTownsville

Professor Utonium accidentally creates three super-powered sisters while trying to make the perfect little girls.

The recipe? Sugar, spice, everything nice… plus Chemical X!

Three tiny heroes. Three different personalities. One powerful team.

06 · Masha and the Bear · 01

Masha and the Bear

🐻 FAMILY

A popular Russian 3D-animated preschool series produced by Animaccord Animation Studio.

Since 20093D animationComedyPreschool

The series follows Masha, an energetic and mischievous four-year-old girl, and Bear, a warm former circus performer who lives peacefully in the forest.

06 · Masha and the Bear · 02

Two characters, opposite personalities

👧 Masha

Curious, energetic, mischievous, and always ready to explore. Her curiosity often turns a normal day into chaos.

🐻 The Bear

Patient, caring, and calm. He often acts as Masha's reluctant but loving caretaker.

🌲 The setting

The forest and Bear's home create a warm world for everyday adventures.

😂 The comedy

The humor often comes from Masha's energy contrasting with Bear's desire for peace and quiet.

06 · Masha and the Bear · 03
